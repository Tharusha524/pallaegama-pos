# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

### Your business need

You run a busy supermarket. Customers come in all day. You sell hundreds of
different items. You buy from many suppliers. Some customers buy on credit.

To stay in control, you need to know three things every day:

1. What did I sell?
2. What stock do I have left?
3. Did I make money?

Doing this by hand takes hours, and the numbers are never fully correct.

### The problems you face today

- Billing is slow and customers wait in long lines
- You do not know your real stock until you walk the shelves
- Items run out without warning, and you lose sales
- Items expire on the shelf, and you lose money
- Credit customers owe money, but nobody knows the correct total
- Cashiers can sell an item too cheap by mistake, and nobody notices
- You only find out your profit at the end of the month — too late to fix it
- If the internet goes down, billing stops completely

### How our POS system solves them

The DIO Smart Supermarket POS System puts your whole shop into one system.
Billing, stock, suppliers, customers and money all work together.

When a cashier scans an item and prints the bill, the system at the same time:

- Reduces the stock
- Records the sale and the profit
- Adds loyalty points for the customer
- Updates the owner's dashboard

Nothing is entered twice. Nothing is forgotten.

### Main benefits

| Benefit | What it means for you |
|---|---|
| **Faster billing** | Scan and print in seconds. Shorter queues. |
| **Billing never stops** | Works fully even when the internet is down. |
| **Real stock control** | Know what you have, right now. |
| **No selling below cost** | The system blocks it automatically. |
| **Clear reports** | See your true profit every day. |
| **Staff accountability** | Know which cashier did what. |

# 2. PROPOSED POS SOLUTION

### What you get

A complete supermarket management system, made of two parts that share the
same information:

**1. The billing counter application (Desktop)**
Installed on the computer at your counter. Built for speed. This is what your
cashiers use all day.

**2. The management system (Web)**
Opened in a normal web browser. This is where you manage products, stock,
suppliers, customers and reports. The owner and the manager use this.

Both work on the same data. A price changed in the office appears at the
counter straight away.

### Setup options

The system can be installed in the way that suits your shop:

| Option | Best for |
|---|---|
| **Local setup** (your own server in the shop) | Shops that want full control and the fastest speed |
| **Cloud setup** (our server) | Shops that want to check their business from home |

### Key points about the system

- **Works offline.** The counter keeps billing even with no internet.
- **Many users at once.** Several counters can bill at the same time.
- **Multi-branch ready.** Move stock between branches and see each branch separately.
- **One central database.** All branches and all counters share the same information.
- **Sinhala supported.** Cashiers can search for items in Sinhala.
- **Customer mobile app (optional).** Your shoppers can see their loyalty
  points and your offers on their phone.

# 3. SYSTEM FEATURES & MODULES

*This is the most important section. Show it as a table or as grouped boxes.*

### 3.1 Sales and Billing

- Barcode scanning
- Search an item by name, item code or barcode
- Search in **Sinhala**
- Full keyboard operation — no mouse needed
- Weighted items (rice, dhal, vegetables) with a weighing scale
- Cash, card, or both split on one bill
- Automatic change calculation
- Hold a bill and come back to it later
- Print a price quotation before the customer decides
- Reprint any old bill
- Cashier shifts — open, close and count the cash drawer
- **Never sell below cost** — the system blocks it
- Every price change by a cashier is recorded

### 3.2 Product and Item Management

- Add items one by one, or **upload hundreds at once from Excel**
- Group items by category and sub-category
- Brands
- Selling price and MRP price
- Print **price labels and barcode stickers**
- Weigh and print labels for loose goods
- Special offer prices with start and end dates

### 3.3 Inventory / Stock Management

- Live stock of every item
- **Low stock alerts** — know before an item runs out
- Record damaged or spoiled goods
- Stock adjustments when the shelf does not match the system
- Adjust many items at once by scanning barcodes
- Stock transfers between branches
- Stock audits — count the shelf and fix the difference
- Batch and expiry tracking — sell the oldest batch first

### 3.4 Purchase Management

- Record every purchase from a supplier
- Direct supplier invoice entry
- Full purchase history
- Stock goes up automatically when you buy

### 3.5 Supplier Management

- Keep a list of all your suppliers
- See what you owe each supplier
- **Lowest cost supplier report** — see who sells each item cheapest

### 3.6 Customer Management

- Add a customer at the counter in seconds
- Search a customer by **name or mobile number**
- Customer groups for targeted offers
- Loyalty points on every purchase
- Loyalty levels (Silver, Gold and so on)
- Points can be used to pay for goods
- Win-back campaigns to bring back customers who stopped coming

### 3.7 Cash and Credit Sales

- Normal cash sales
- Credit sales for approved customers
- A **credit limit** for each customer — the system blocks a sale over the limit
- See exactly how much each customer owes
- **Take credit payments at the billing counter** — no need to go to the office
- The customer can pay old bills only, or old bills with today's shopping
- Full payment or part payment
- The oldest bill is always paid first
- A proper receipt is printed every time

### 3.8 Returns and Refunds

- Process a customer return
- Stock comes back in
- Money goes back out
- Everything recorded correctly

### 3.9 Discounts, Offers and Vouchers

- Discount on one item or on the whole bill
- Special prices for chosen dates
- Discount coupons
- **Gift vouchers** — sell them and accept them
- The system tracks how much voucher money is still unused

### 3.10 User and Role Management

- A separate login for every staff member
- Control which screens each person can open
- A record of every login — who came in, and when
- Cashier shifts with cash counting

### 3.11 Dashboard

One screen that shows the owner:

- Today's sales
- Number of bills issued today
- Money customers owe you
- Money you owe suppliers
- Low stock items
- Sales trend chart
- Best selling items chart

### 3.12 Reports

**More than 18 ready reports. No Excel work needed.**

**Sales and profit**
- Sales summary — total sales, bill count, average bill
- Product profit — which items actually make money
- Business activity
- Sales by cashier and by shift

**Stock**
- Stock valuation — how much money sits on your shelves
- Fast moving items (velocity and demand)
- Dead stock — items not moving
- Expiry list — what is going bad soon
- Negative stock
- Stock history
- Stock by batch

**Money owed**
- Receivables aging — who owes you, and for how long
- Payables aging — who you owe, and for how long
- Voucher liability

**Checking and control**
- Void report — cancelled bills
- Price override audit — who changed a price
- Supplier item list
- Lowest cost supplier

**Accounting**
- General ledger reports and financial statements

### 3.13 Barcode and Receipt Printing

- Works with normal barcode scanners
- Thermal receipt printer support
- Put your logo, name, address and phone on the receipt
- Change the words on every line of the receipt
- Make the receipt shorter to save paper
- Show the customer's loyalty points and credit balance

### 3.14 Offline Operation

- Billing continues with no internet
- Receipts still print
- Credit payments can still be taken
- Everything is saved safely on the counter computer
- When the internet comes back, everything uploads by itself
- Nothing is lost, and nothing is entered twice
