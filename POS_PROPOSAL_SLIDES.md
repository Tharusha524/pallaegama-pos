## SLIDE 2 — The Problem Today

**Running a supermarket by hand is hard.**

- Billing is slow. Customers wait in long lines.
- You do not know what stock you have until you walk the shelves.
- Items run out without warning. You lose sales.
- Items expire on the shelf. You lose money.
- Credit customers owe money, but nobody knows the real total.
- You only find out your profit at the end of the month — too late to fix it.

## SLIDE 4 — How It All Fits Together

**The flow of your shop, inside the system.**

1. **You buy goods** → enter the purchase → stock goes up
2. **You set the price** → selling price and MRP
3. **Customer buys** → scan barcode → bill printed → stock goes down
4. **System records** → the sale, the profit, the customer, the money
5. **You check reports** → see what sold, what is left, what you earned

## SLIDE 5 — The Billing Counter (POS)

**Built for speed. Made for busy cashiers.**

- Scan the barcode — the item is added at once
- No barcode? Type the name, the item code, or part of it
- Search in **Sinhala** too
- Use the keyboard only — no mouse needed
- Quantity first or price first — the cashier chooses
- Weighted items (rice, vegetables) work with your weighing scale
- Cash, card, or split between both on one bill
- Change is worked out for you
- Hold a bill and come back to it later (customer forgot something)
- Print a **price quotation** before the customer decides

---

## SLIDE 6 — Smart Protection at the Counter

**The system stops mistakes before they cost you money.**

- **Never sell below cost.** If a cashier types a price under your buying price, the system blocks it.
- **Price change tracking.** Every time a cashier changes a price, it is recorded. You can see who did it.
- **Out of stock warning.** The cashier is told before the sale, not after.
- **Expiry and batch control.** Sell the oldest batch first.
- **Returns handled properly.** Stock comes back, money goes back, everything recorded.

*Speaker note: This slide usually wins the owner over. Mistakes at the counter are their biggest silent loss.*

**You never have to turn a customer away.**

## SLIDE 12 — Credit Customers

**Give credit safely.**

- Keep a list of customers who buy on credit
- Set a credit limit for each one
- The system blocks a sale that goes over the limit
- See exactly how much each customer owes
- **Take credit payments right at the billing counter** — the customer does not have to go to the office
- The customer can pay old bills only, or old bills together with today's shopping
- Full payment or part payment — both work
- A proper receipt is printed every time
- The system always pays the oldest bill first
